A busy bot face performs now, and the chat stops lagging behind the list.

Bot face: every busy state jumps and flips between its acting — crouch, leap, squash-and-stretch landing, then a 360° roll or an out-and-back 180° twist, one stunt every ~2.6-3 s, direction alternating so no face spins one way forever. The eyes act faster than the body: a saccade every ~0.9 s, a two-step double take every fifth, a squint on a slower clock. Two faces never perform in lockstep, an instant always draws the same face, and idle, waiting and error stay exactly as they were. Reduce Motion and the Appearance switch still stop everything; the leap is capped in points so a 16 pt face never draws over the text beside it.

Chat wiring: a reply the bot list and the notification already announced is now in the chat when you open it. The app used to trust a two-minute-old cached conversation and skip the host read entirely, because it assumed anything new would arrive on the live socket — but a reply for a bot that is not on screen is dropped, not buffered. Now an unseen session, or a host message newer than the snapshot's, forces the read; and an idle open chat re-reads itself when the host has written, which also covers messages that produce no socket event at all: another device's turn, an agent posting outside a run, a bot-to-bot transfer. Nothing new still comes from the cache, instantly.
