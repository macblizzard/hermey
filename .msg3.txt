test(chat): give the fixture its transcript, and put the read mark behind the host stamp

The first CI run of `StaleConversationTests` failed every case at its first assertion, and it was the
fixture, not the fix: the helper took `entries` and never passed them to `ScriptedHost`, so each test
opened an empty conversation and then asserted about rows that were never there. Passing them through
is the whole repair for four of the five failures.

The fifth was a dishonest test: a host stamp of `2000` is 1970, so the sweep could never call it newer
than the read mark the model writes on open, and "the list learned about it — that is the banner"
would have passed for the wrong reason or failed for one. The test now moves the mark behind the host's
stamp explicitly (`markRead(at: 1000)`), which is what the real disagreement looks like.

Also records the wiring fix in CHANGELOG under Unreleased → Fixed.
