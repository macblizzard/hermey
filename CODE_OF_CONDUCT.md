# Code of conduct

Be decent. In practice:

- Assume competence and good faith; ask before assuming a mistake.
- Critique code, not people.
- No harassment, discrimination or personal attacks, in issues, PRs or anywhere else the project
  lives.
- Respect a maintainer's decision to close a discussion, even when you disagree.

Violations can be reported privately to the maintainer; they will be handled discreetly. Repeated or
serious violations lead to a ban from the project spaces.
